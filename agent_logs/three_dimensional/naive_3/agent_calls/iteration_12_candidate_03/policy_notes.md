## Policy hypothesis

Return to the sampled `2.443L` alignment-gated carrier and add one mechanism:
a smooth near-target posterior half-cycle asymmetry. Normalized body-frame
lateral target error selects the requested side. Joint state reconstructs the
lagged posterior wave, and only the half-cycle already bending opposite the
requested anterior turn receives an additional bounded posterior target bias.
This creates a transient S-bend once per beat while fully releasing on the
other half-cycle, preserving the cruise waveform and avoiding the failed
geometry-persistent equilibrium.

Expected evidence is unchanged far-field displacement and wake coherence,
then a visibly asymmetric terminal beat that redirects targetward without
continuous coasting. Capture, a useful new termination, or a minimum below
`2.187L` without degraded mean distance supports the mechanism. Falsify it if
the cruise trajectory changes, the wake becomes a tight curl or loses its
traveling pattern, posterior limit/load residence rises materially, or the
same powered lower exit persists without a useful distance improvement.
