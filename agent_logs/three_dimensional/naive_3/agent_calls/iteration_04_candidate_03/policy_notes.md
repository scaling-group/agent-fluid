# Wake-policy candidate notes

## Inherited and sampled evidence

- All four sampled evaluations satisfy the frozen experiment: direct uniform
  `U_infinity=(0,0,0)` initialization, no cylinders, and no prewarm. Their
  motion and wakes are generated by the policies rather than ambient
  advection.
- The strongest finite sample is the bearing-minus-body-slip posterior
  curvature controller. Its top-down row shows a coherent alternating wake
  from about `5T` through `26T`, and its oblique row confirms persistent
  three-dimensional Lambda2 structures rather than a planar rendering
  artifact. It self-propels from `x=21.000L` to `10.217L` by the closest
  approach at `17.70T`; distance falls from `12.328L` to `2.960L` while center
  `y` initially falls toward the target from `14.000L` to `12.324L`.
- That useful trajectory is an overshoot, not capture. Bearing has already
  grown to about `-0.87 rad` at `16T` and reaches `-1.50 rad`; after the
  `2.960L` closest approach the fish continues left, turns upward, and exits
  at center `y=15.200L` and distance `7.786L` at `26.64T`. The tail's raw
  acceleration request exceeds `1800 deg/T^2` in about `64.7%` of samples and
  its rate touches `260 deg/T` in about `7.2%`, so a larger static curvature
  or carrier is unlikely to create clean corrective authority.
- The sampled bearing/trend mean-curvature controller and posterior
  opposing-half-cycle relief controller both retain coherent 3D wakes but
  exit the upper boundary at `13.13T` and `12.55T`, with closest distances
  `9.141L` and `9.855L`. Thus tail half-cycle attenuation applied throughout
  target turning changed rate/effort but did not improve containment.
- The prefilled phase-referenced yaw-rate servo changes the failure topology:
  center `y` stays within `13.816--14.293L` and the fish reaches the left
  boundary only after `26.33T`. But it never converts that containment into
  adequate descent, passes the target's x station roughly `4.4L` too high,
  and bottoms out at `4.361L`. Its raw posterior acceleration demand is worse
  (`76.0%` above the envelope), so further residual-yaw fitting is not the
  best-supported next mechanism.
- The assigned parent guidance and inherited notes therefore support keeping
  the zero-centered anterior traveling-bend carrier, using the best sampled
  bearing/slip mapping for broad target-directed travel, and changing how
  posterior authority is allocated only after useful approach is established.

## Policy hypothesis

Retain the strongest sample's anterior oscillator, posterior lag, and bounded
bearing-minus-lateral-slip mean curvature. Add one continuous approach
allocation mechanism: while far away or aligned, leave the demonstrated tail
carrier unchanged; when normalized distance is small and absolute body-frame
bearing is large, symmetrically attenuate only the oscillatory posterior
carrier while retaining the mean steering tangent. This should lower
overshoot speed and free joint-angle/rate/acceleration reserve for corrective
curvature without shifting the anterior oscillator center, increasing peak
steering, selecting a world-frame route, or depending on time.

The next evaluation should reproduce the strong sample's coherent far-field
wake and progress through about `5L`, then show reduced tail effort and a turn
toward the target before passing its x station. Falsify the mechanism if the
far-field wake or progress collapses, attenuation activates while far or
aligned, tail-limit occupancy does not fall in the misaligned approach, or the
trajectory fails to beat `2.960L` and repeats an upper-boundary exit.
