# Wake-policy candidate notes

## Evidence diagnosis before the policy edit

- All four sampled rollouts used direct uniform initialization in still water
  with `U_infinity=(0,0,0)`, no cylinders, and no prewarm. Their motion and
  wake are therefore generated by the fish rather than advection or inherited
  flow.
- I inspected both rows of the combined keyframe sheets for the strongest
  sampled response-gated brake (`2.385L` minimum) and the prefilled
  joint-phase counterbend failure (`2.512L`), then also checked the inherited
  response-selected counterstroke failure (`2.585L`). In every top-down row,
  the fish builds a strong alternating vorticity street and follows the same
  diagonal approach before turning nearly vertical below the target. The
  oblique rows show a coherent three-dimensional Lambda2 chain through the
  approach and lower-boundary exit. There is no visible passive advection,
  collision, wake collapse, or instability; all three failures remain powered
  and differ mainly in terminal trajectory geometry.
- The sampled metrics agree with the images. The plain alignment carrier,
  response brake, phase counterbend, and response-released equilibrium S-bend
  all terminate by lower exit near `31T`, with minima of `2.443`, `2.385`,
  `2.512`, and `2.536L` and nearly identical final distances of
  `9.190--9.207L`. At the best brake's minimum near `17.87T`, the full
  body-frame direction error is `1.379 rad`, error-growing raw yaw is
  `2.185 rad/T`, closure is approximately zero, and translational speed is
  still `0.705U`. Anterior/posterior accelerations are clamped for about
  `0.747/0.356` of its samples, so more drive or a command-bound change is not
  supported.
- The assigned parent's inherited logs close off two tempting posterior
  continuations. Reallocating the brake's discarded wave authority into an
  opposite-sign equilibrium residual reached only `2.444L`, and reversing it
  as a response-selected counterstroke reached `2.585L`; both retained the
  powered lower exit. Those results, plus the sampled joint-phase counterbend,
  are concrete negative evidence against another posterior reallocation or
  half-cycle-strength edit.
- The same inherited analysis reports that raw yaw is phase-locked to anterior
  joint velocity inside `3L`. Recalculation over the four sampled traces gives
  correlations of `-0.9925` to `-0.9978` and regression slopes of
  `-0.399` to `-0.459`. Thus raw yaw is a useful measured half-cycle selector
  for the posterior brake, but it is contaminated by the propulsive rhythm
  when the anterior mean-curvature loop treats it as slow course response. A
  normalized residual `heading_rate + 0.45*phi_dot1` is the common near-target
  cancellation hypothesis; the coefficient comes from these rollouts, not
  from a published animal or robot gain.

## Policy hypothesis

Start from the sampled `2.385L` response-gated brake, preserving its
state-feedback oscillator, bounded target-relative curvature, posterior lag,
alignment envelope, measured raw-yaw half-cycle selector, command reserve, and
far-field cruise. During the existing smooth approach envelope only, subtract
the gait-synchronous yaw component inferred from anterior joint velocity
before raw yaw enters the anterior mean-curvature damping term. The posterior
brake still sees raw measured yaw, so the new mechanism changes course
estimation rather than repeating a posterior counterbend.

The expected result is unchanged far-field progress and coherent traveling
wake, followed by a different anterior mean-curvature history as the fish
enters the lateral near miss. Capture, a useful new termination class, or a
minimum materially below `2.385L` without worse mean progress would support
the mechanism. Falsify it if far-field motion changes, the wake becomes
one-sided or collapses, a tight curl appears, clamp/load residence grows, or
the same powered lower exit and minimum persist. A failure would mean the
joint-rate/yaw correlation is only descriptive, not a useful course-response
residual, and later workers should not tune its coefficient without a new
semantic trajectory.

## Implemented candidate and non-CFD checks

The candidate implements the proposed residual with one rollout-calibrated
parameter, `gait_yaw_joint_rate_gain=0.45`, under the existing approach
envelope. It otherwise starts from the sampled response-gated brake. In an
action-local state reconstructed at that rollout's minimum, the residual
changes the two commands by approximately `(1.542, 1.545) rad/T^2`; at the
same geometry scaled to `8L`, the changes are only `(0.0020, 0.0026)`, so the
mechanism is active near the diagnosed miss and negligible in cruise. These
are static policy replays, not coupled-flow predictions.

The mandated material-guidance/schema check, lightweight Julia contract, and
solver-boundary check pass. All `324` repository non-CFD assertions pass. A
grid of `14,580` mirrored state pairs remains finite and within the configured
`28 rad/T^2` command reserve, with zero numerical reflection error in the two
commands. Formal CFD was not run.
