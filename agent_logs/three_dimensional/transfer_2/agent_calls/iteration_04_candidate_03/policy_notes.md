## Policy hypothesis

Use one normalized geometric steering mechanism: after forward motion develops,
compute the signed cross-product between body-frame unit target direction and
body-frame inertial course; at release, continuously fall back to target
lateral direction because course is undefined. Map that bounded error to the
3D-calibrated distributed mean bend used by the coherent compact controller:
positive lateral error creates positive mean tail tangent plus a small
opposite anterior acceleration. Keep joint state as the only gait phase,
retain posterior lag, and smoothly bound both acceleration commands.

Expected result: the fish should retain an organized traveling wake, initially
correct its small positive target error, then respond to the large negative
target/course mismatch before passing above the target. A useful outcome must
beat the upper-exit topology through a smaller closest distance or a better
termination class, without increasing speed-limit residence or load spikes.
Falsify the mechanism if it curls upward before forward course is established,
repeats the upper exit without contracting target/course mismatch, collapses
the coherent wake, or raises joint/command saturation.
