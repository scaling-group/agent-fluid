## Candidate policy hypothesis

from the assigned parent. Preserve the oscillator, carrier-response residual,
base target/course redirect, posterior traveling wave and relief, anterior
corridor release, acceleration allocation, and exact-boundary projection.
Release only supplemental yaw-residual posterior curvature when a closing
approach is inside a small normalized body-frame bearing cone and the measured
bearing magnitude is reopening; restore it continuously outside the cone.

all cruise behavior, and the coherent alternating wake while improving the
terminal crossing and distance integral relative to corridor-only release.

loses capture, disrupts either wake view, regresses final/integrated distance,
or raises limiting or loads enough to outweigh the crossing benefit. This
worker does not claim its downstream evaluation as current evidence.
