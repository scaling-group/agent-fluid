## Single candidate hypothesis

Restore the evidenced a4eae6d626b3 traveling-bend carrier, shared-joint
half-cycle actuation, and smooth acceleration envelope. Replace its
instantaneous body-lateral wrong-side-slip trigger with one course-divergence
mechanism: compute the signed cross product between the normalized body-frame
target direction and body-frame translational velocity, then gate both the
course correction and the higher redirect authority by the bounded magnitude
of bearing * course_crossflow. The product is reflection invariant and is
small when either geometric error or course misalignment is small, avoiding
early beat-scale course reversals. When both remain large, the signed course
residual reinforces the bearing request and keeps the stronger but sub-unity
half-cycle redirect active even if instantaneous body-lateral velocity changes
sign. No rate barrier or carrier scalar tuning is included, so this rollout
isolates the response gate.

Falsify the transfer if the upper-boundary termination persists without a
meaningful closest-approach or horizon gain, if the correction turns away from
the target, or if alternating wake coherence and leftward propulsion collapse.
Also compare joint-rate occupancy and force/moment peaks against the best
sample; semantic improvement obtained only by more persistent hard-limit use
is not robust evidence.
