- The successful candidate still reaches the acceleration and rate envelopes
  transiently, so this evidence does not establish general actuator margin.
  It does establish a stable capture trajectory with lower terminal angle

## Candidate hypothesis and selection

Use the completed `solver_a1253ad45bc8` terminal curvature-reallocation policy
as the one downstream candidate. Preserve the parent's large-error
geometry-to-curvature redirect and posterior-lag state-feedback carrier. Only
inside the normalized proximity band, and only while the existing body-frame
angle requests redirect, convexly reallocate most shared two-joint authority
from oscillation to a separately bounded, critically damped mean-curvature
tracker. Retain a small carrier floor and release continuously as proximity or
misalignment falls. No clock, route, world coordinate, target identity, or
vortex phase is introduced.

This is an evidence-led exploitation candidate rather than an unevaluated
combination with approach damping. Expected evidence is a coherent far wake
and capture near `25.26T`. Falsify its reuse if the new coupled rollout loses
capture, materially changes the trajectory before `4L`, stalls outside the
`0.75L` radius, develops persistent joint-limit dwell, or produces terminal
force/moment spikes comparable to the parent's post-miss loop.
