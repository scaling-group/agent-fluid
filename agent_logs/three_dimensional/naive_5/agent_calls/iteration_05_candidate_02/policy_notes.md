  evaluations: `U_infinity=(0,0,0)`, no cylinders, no prewarm, and an inertial
  moving window. Their top-down translation and oblique Lambda2 trails are
  generated by self-propulsion rather than background advection.
- The combined sheets show a coherent alternating wake in every sampled long
  rollout. The assigned-parent descendant `solver_aaab22dcaa09` preserves that
  wake through `38.671T`, moves its late path down to `y=13.990L`, and attains
  the best sampled minimum distance, `4.676L`. The prefilled

  nearly horizontal path stays around `y=14.7L`, reaches only `5.264L`, and
  exits left. Thus the parent's evidence-calibrated opposite selector sign

- The highest scalar sample, `solver_12fc3441a636`, is an informative failure
  rather than a target-reaching solution. Its top-down wake bends upward and
  reaches the upper boundary at `20.790T`; distance happens to improve
  monotonically to `6.268L` at termination. Its inherited course/rate selector
  is positive while negative bearing requires recovery in the opposite yaw
  direction, so its score does not validate that actuator mapping.
- Instantaneous yaw is not removed by a velocity-only phase model. Least-squares
  fits after `2T` for the four current traces give the repeatable relation
  `heading_rate = k_v*phi_dot1 + k_q*phi1 + slow residual`, with
  `k_v=-0.455,-0.455,-0.481,-0.459` and
  `k_q=1.078,1.131,1.214,1.102`. After `8T`, subtracting only the velocity
  component leaves the putative slow-yaw residual `0.977` to `0.989`
  correlated with `phi1` and with standard deviation `0.083` to `0.085`.
  Removing both normalized quadratures with common coefficients reduces that
  standard deviation to `0.010` to `0.015`. Reconstructing the parent's
  selector on its own trace then changes it from alternating sign on `41.3%`
  of samples to the requested negative side throughout the negative-bearing
  interval, without increasing a steering gain.
- The soft angle/speed headroom in `solver_65e67137c3f9` is worth preserving:
  it has no anterior angle samples above `44 deg` and `40.9%` speed occupancy
  above `250 deg/T`, compared with `56.1%` speed occupancy for the assigned
  parent. The remaining acceleration-clamp occupancy is dominated by the
  posterior tracker, so the candidate does not add raw drive.

## Policy hypothesis

Preserve the evidenced traveling-bend carrier, symmetric phase pump, lagged
posterior follower, and joint headroom envelope. Estimate slow yaw by
normalizing anterior joint angle and velocity as two phase quadratures and
subtracting both repeatable gait-synchronous components from normalized body
yaw. Close the target-bearing request on that residual using the assigned
parent's empirically calibrated error-to-half-stroke sign: when bearing is
negative and observed slow yaw is insufficiently positive, select the negative
anterior half-stroke. This is a phase-observer change, not scalar-only gain
tuning.

Expected result: the alternating 3D wake and leftward propulsion should remain,
while the selector holds a coherent recovery side long enough to bend the
trajectory downward before the target's x station. The candidate should beat
the parent's `4.676L` closest approach or improve the termination class without
raising joint-limit residence. Falsify the mechanism if the two-quadrature
residual remains gait-correlated, the trajectory stays above `y=14L` at target
passage, the upper-exit topology returns, wake coherence or translation
collapses, or speed/acceleration saturation exceeds the parent.
