# Candidate wake-policy notes

## Evidence diagnosis

- The only sampled parent is both the strongest finite example available and
  the informative failure. It used direct uniform still-water initialization,
  no prewarm, and no cylinders, so the motion and wake are generated by the
  fish rather than inherited advection.
- In both visual rows, the seed develops a coherent alternating tail wake by
  `2T` and retains three-dimensional Lambda2 structure. It therefore has a
  useful traveling-bend propulsion carrier. The top-down sequence also shows
  the fish curling upward instead of holding its initially target-aligned
  course; the turn is pronounced by `8T`, immediately before upper-boundary
  exit.
- The trace confirms that failure topology. Center motion is
  `(21.000,14.000)L -> (20.075,15.200)L`, heading changes from `0.506` to
  `-0.782 rad`, and peak absolute heading rate is `2.792 rad/T`. Distance only
  falls from `12.328L` to `12.078L` at `6.358T`, then rises to `12.380L` before
  `left_domain` at `8.547T`.
- This is not merely weak propulsion. Joint angles reach about `26.6 deg`, but
  raw policy acceleration exceeds the `1800 deg/T^2` actuator envelope on
  `52.4%` of trace samples (maximum `75.37 rad/T^2`), and joint-speed clipping
  occurs on `4.4%`. A steering increment added to that carrier would often be
  masked by the actuator clamp.
- No inherited optimizer note exists in this fresh workspace. The assigned
  guidance therefore contributes the durable constraints: retain the useful
  state-feedback oscillator, introduce normalized body-frame target feedback,
  and avoid route, time, or world-coordinate commands.

## Policy hypothesis

Preserve the seed's state-derived anterior oscillator and lagged posterior
wave, but give the posterior target a bounded mean offset computed from
body-frame bearing. Normalize measured heading rate by gait angular frequency
and oppose an already-developing turn, so the offset behaves as a damped target
curvature request rather than a fixed bias. Relax the overdriven carrier enough
to restore acceleration headroom; this supportive change is justified by the
measured clipping, not by a literature gain. The resulting policy remains a
memoryless two-joint state-feedback law with no clock or route state.

Expected result: early target-side curvature should arrest the seed's
uncommanded clockwise curl, keep the fish inside the virtual field beyond
`8.55T`, and produce sustained distance decrease while preserving an
alternating posterior wake. The post-rollout test must also check that raw
acceleration clipping falls materially below `52.4%`.
