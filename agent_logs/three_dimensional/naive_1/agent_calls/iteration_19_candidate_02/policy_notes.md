# Candidate diagnosis and hypothesis

## Evidence diagnosis before policy editing

- All four sampled solver examples satisfy direct-uniform still-water
  initialization and capture. Their combined sheets show genuine
  self-propulsion: a coherent alternating top-down vorticity street remains
  attached to a broad target-directed turn, and the oblique row retains
  compact paired caudal Lambda2 structures. The geometry-scheduled variants
  form a replicated capture band at `18.6505--18.6835T` and mean distance
  `2.09340--2.09405L`.
- The prefilled half-cycle envelope redistribution initially appeared useful:
  it captured at `18.8265T`, improved mean distance to `2.08855L`, and kept
  both wake views coherent. Inherited step-18 results overturn a positive
  repeatability claim. The identical executable policy captured once at
  `19.0520T` and `2.09871L`, but another repeat missed the `0.75L` radius at
  `0.81206L`, then continued below the target and exited at `34.2320T` with
  final distance `10.6779L`. Its coherent wake therefore does not establish
  reliable route allocation.
- The inherited response-gated redistribution is a stronger negative result:
  it kept an energetic alternating top-down street and compact caudal 3D
  structures, yet bent high, approached only `2.47664L`, and exited at
  `31.7570T`. Do not stack another beat-scale release gate onto this fragile
  redistribution.
- The informative near-miss is terminal rather than propulsive. At its closest
  row (`19.1895T`), the head is at `(9.5621,8.9140)L`, moving left/down with
  planar speed about `0.85L/T`; distance is `0.81206L`, body-frame target
  error remains strongly lateral, and target-signed yaw is already correcting.
  The existing one-sided response gate consequently releases up to `35%` of
  mean curvature just when the fish must bend back toward the target. The wake
  remains coherent and load peaks stay inside captured comparison ranges, so
  more carrier effort or a wake-disturbance residual is not supported.

## Single-candidate policy hypothesis

Return to the repeatedly captured geometry-scheduled carrier and add one
continuous approach-authority mechanism. Far from the target, preserve its
evidenced target-signed differential curvature, displacement-only half-cycle
steering, common amplitude relief, posterior lag, and final acceleration
projection exactly. Inside a parameter-owned body-length approach scale,
continuously suppress the correcting-yaw *release fraction*, restoring the
original target-signed curvature request as distance closes. This mechanism
cannot invert the route command and does not change rhythmic amplitude,
posterior allocation, or beat phase. It should leave the established far-field
route and both wake views intact while turning the observed `0.812L` overshoot
into capture or a bounded reacquisition. Falsify it if capture repeatability is
not restored, the route leaves the geometry-carrier band before the approach
region, either wake loses coherence, closest approach worsens, or saturation
and planar loads materially exceed the captured range.
