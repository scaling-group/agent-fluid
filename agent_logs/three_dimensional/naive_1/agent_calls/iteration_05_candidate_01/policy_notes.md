This supports retaining ungated mean curvature but adding response-dependent authority, rather than another bearing gate, posterior bias, or propulsion-only gain edit.

Falsify it if the alternating wake is lost, rate/load saturation worsens, closest approach exceeds `5.033L`, or the trajectory still crosses the target-side corridor and diverges to the lower boundary without reducing its bearing/yaw oscillation.
