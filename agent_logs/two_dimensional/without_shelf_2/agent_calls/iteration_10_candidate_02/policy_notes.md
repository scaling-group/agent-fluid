successful steering, period, amplitude, posterior target, lag, and damping.
Change only `oscillator_energy_gain` from `2.0` to `1.8`. Unlike changing the
hard guard, this preserves the nominal unit-energy orbit and its restoring
frequency while weakening only the rate at which amplitude errors caused by
release and wake disturbances are pumped or damped. The candidate tests
whether the current energy recovery is converting transient energy error into
guard contact and route sensitivity; it does not assume that lower authority
or lower steady gait amplitude is beneficial.

upstream-relative propulsion while reducing command effort, load, or guard

force/moment do not rise above `22.39/393.08`. Reject the change if oscillator

would still require falsification across held-out wake phase, inflow, geometry,
and target placement; no outcome for this unevaluated candidate is claimed.
